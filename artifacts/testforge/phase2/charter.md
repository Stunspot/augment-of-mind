# MIND Phase 2 associative disclosure verification charter

## Decision under test

Whether the bounded Phase 2 source change is fit to remain the persona-neutral,
privacy-preserving associative reminder substrate, and whether its portable
delivery envelope can be consumed by a cooperative host without inflating the
evidence ceiling to live automatic delivery.

## Defining risks

- private capability existence or counts leak before filtering;
- a profile, time, or revision fork escapes the globally ordered generation;
- canonical and compact forms silently change membership;
- replay, drift, or token reuse accepts a different field or card revision;
- lexical/vector/bridge membership becomes an ordered recommendation list;
- raw prompt, anchor, vector, or field material is persisted;
- the delivery envelope is altered or credited beyond construction evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Repository-local deterministic tests and source inspection can establish the
bounded implementation and portable contract. They cannot establish observed
Codex Desktop ingestion, authenticated provider delivery, a live H1 receipt,
fresh-host installation, or public release readiness.
