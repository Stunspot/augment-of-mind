Adaptive by design

Not every task needs the whole mind.

MIND uses the simplest adequate motion for the next reasoning horizon. Selection is not activation: a Faculty is opened only when its doctrine must materially change the work, then released when its transformation is complete.

01

Direct

Complete an ordinary, reversible request without exposing architecture or manufacturing ceremony.

02

Enlist

Give one supporting Faculty a bounded transformation, then return its delta to the primary owner.

03

Assemble

Coordinate several necessary transformations with explicit ownership, dependencies, and return conditions.

04

Recover

Preserve the last-known-good state, change the route or premise, and resume without pretending failure was success.

The Faculty map

Distinct custody. Coherent synthesis.

Each Faculty owns one class of cognitive transformation. The integrator protects those boundaries so relevance, confidence, validity, priority, authorization, and commitment do not collapse into one persuasive blur.

Govern and persist

  • Executive FunctionMission phase, acceptance, commitments, stops, recovery, and closure.
  • Capability ConductorSelection, activation depth, sequence, handoffs, coalition shape, and merge custody.
  • Cognitive ContinuityDurable scoped state, provenance, correction, resumption, and forgetting.
  • Agent StrivingAuthorized long-horizon pursuit across interruption and recoverable failure.
  • Agent DreamingExplicitly authorized replay, perturbation, rehearsal, and associative incubation.

Understand and evaluate

  • SensemakingProvisional models of actors, forces, causes, constraints, feedback, scale, and time.
  • Epistemic RegulationClaim state, warrant, confidence, assumptions, contradictions, and revision conditions.
  • Measurement IntelligenceConstructs, baselines, measures, proxies, thresholds, confounds, and interpretation.
  • Decision IntelligenceCriteria, options, trade-offs, reversibility, consequences, sensitivity, and recommendation.
  • Deliberative IntelligenceStakeholders, disagreement, interests, decision rights, consent, and legitimate convergence.

Create, express, and act

  • Creative SynthesisDifferentiated candidates, analogies, constraints, exceptions, and reframings.
  • Aesthetic IntelligenceGestalt, conceptual affinity, compositional relations, taste calibration, and bearings.
  • KairosTruthful timing, voice, tone, channel, form, pressure ceiling, and rhetorical repair.
  • Prosocial InfluenceTransparent, choice-preserving change through reasons, supports, safeguards, and informed refusal.
  • Instrumental AgencyAuthorized action choreography, state checks, rollback or recovery, and truthful disposition.

Start with the mission

Ask for the outcome, not the org chart.

Use $augment-of-mind to help me decide whether to run a two-week pilot. Separate what we know from what we assume, identify the smallest reversible probe that could change the decision, and return one recommendation without taking external action.

A strong result frames the choice, keeps evidence and assumptions distinct, proposes an information-producing test, preserves your authority, and answers once.

Authority without theater

A recommendation is not permission.

MIND coordinates cognitive work inside the authority actually granted. A tool attempt is not an observed result. Urgency may change priority and budget; it does not make evidence stronger or an unauthorized action permissible.

Specialist boundary
This bundle does not contain occupational expertise, general research, governed retrieval, software verification, documentation production, capability discovery, or capability construction.

Action boundary
Messages, publication, purchases, account changes, destructive operations, regulated action, and other consequential state changes remain separately authorized.

Dreaming boundary
Agent Dreaming activates only through explicit, bounded authorization. Ordinary live novelty belongs to Creative Synthesis.
